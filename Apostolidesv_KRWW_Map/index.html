Understanding the Data

E. coli: An indicator of fecal contamination in surface water.

pH: Measures how acidic or basic the water is.

Dissolved Oxygen: Measures oxygen available in water for aquatic organisms.

Temperature: Influences aquatic ecosystems, oxygen solubility, and biological processes.
